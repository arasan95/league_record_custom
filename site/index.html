LeagueRecord Custom 日本語

Open-source Windows desktop app

Record locally. Upload only when you choose.

LeagueRecord Custom records League of Legends gameplay on your Windows PC. Its optional YouTube feature lets you upload a finished recording or clip that you select to your own authorized YouTube channel.

View source on GitHub How Google data is used
Local by default

Recording and video management happen on your computer. LeagueRecord Custom does not upload a video unless you select it and explicitly start the upload.

Your channel, your settings

You connect a Google account in the system browser and confirm the title, description, visibility, audience setting, and thumbnail before uploading.

YouTube API use

LeagueRecord Custom uses YouTube API Services for user-authorized video uploads, custom thumbnails, upload-processing checks, and availability checks for videos shared through the app. It does not collect or analyze a user's general YouTube activity.
