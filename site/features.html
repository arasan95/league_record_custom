Features

What it can do

The main features available in LeagueRecord Custom.

01

Match recording

Detects the start of a League of Legends match and records it. Recordings are stored on your PC.

02

Clips

Save a selected part of a recording. Game audio and voice chat can also be separated after recording.

03

Markers

Shows kills, objectives, and other events on the timeline so you can move directly to that time.

04

Tooltips

View champion ability and item information inside the application.

05

Site links

Open match history, Wiki, matchup, and build websites from the scoreboard.

06

13 languages

Change the display language used for the interface and tooltips.

07

Hotkeys

Control recording, clips, and other actions with configurable hotkeys.

08

Filters

Filter recordings and clips by selected conditions.

09

YouTube sharing

Enter a YouTube link in the application to load shared match data and play the video with markers.

10

Shared comments

Users watching the same video can share comments connected to a time in the video.
